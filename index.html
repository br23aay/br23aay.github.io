Reinforcement learning for dexterous in-hand manipulation

First-authored study of a 24-DoF Shadow Dexterous Hand learning to rotate a pen with Proximal Policy Optimisation, phase-based reward shaping, and tactile feedback. The policy was caught exploiting the reward, then corrected. Results held across three training seeds.
