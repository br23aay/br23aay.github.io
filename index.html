Published Work

Peer-Reviewed Research

First-authored RL research on dexterous in-hand manipulation — published April 2025 in IJRES, Impact Factor 7.52.

IJRES VOL.13 · ISSUE 6 · PP.164–183 · APRIL 2025 · IMPACT FACTOR 7.52

Reinforcement Learning for Robot Dexterous
In-Hand Manipulation (Shadow Hand)

Trained a 24-degree-of-freedom Shadow Dexterous Hand to perform in-hand pen rotation using PPO, phase-based reward shaping, and tactile sensor feedback. Discovered and mitigated reward hacking behaviour during training — the policy learned to exploit the reward signal rather than completing the task, then was corrected through redesigned reward decomposition. Results reproduced across 3 independent training seeds. Co-authored with supervisor Dr Diego Resende Faria.
